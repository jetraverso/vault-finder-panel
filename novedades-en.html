1.27.0 latest September 3, 2026
  • New icon: the drawing in white on the logo's violet, with a soft glow from the centre
1.26.0 September 3, 2026
  • In Results, the active-filters bar (FILTERS) moved below the list, right above the selection bar and "Create vault"
1.25.0 September 3, 2026
  • Videos start muted, both the preview and the one opened with a double-click. Turn the sound on with the speaker in the playback bar or with the video's floating control
  • The trim buttons now move as many seconds as you choose: next to "ALL" there is a dropdown (±1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 10 or 15 s) and all four buttons follow it. It is remembered between sessions
  • ⌘ click on the preview clips builds a group: the ±N buttons, "Reset it" and the ⌫ key act on every clip in the group. A plain click clears it
  • "ALL" button at the start of the trim bar: when on, the ±N buttons act on every clip in the preview
1.24.0 August 24, 2026
  • My filters: save a combination with a name and apply it again in one click. They live in the left column of the filters step and are kept between sessions
  • New filter opens a window to build it by picking from the lists: the actions you choose add up between them, and qualifiers, attributes and cameras narrow it down. It covers cases like "shots on goal from one sector" or "by one player assisted by another". While you build it, it tells you how many actions it catches
  • Fixed: at the start of the clip the buttons were the wrong way round. Now "−5s" starts five seconds earlier (and lengthens) and "+5s" starts later (and shortens), just like at the end
1.23.0 August 24, 2026
  • Fixed: the order you set in the preview is now the order of the exported timeline. "Group by action" was on and reordered on top of whatever you had arranged by hand
  • As soon as you move a clip, your order takes over and the preview says so: next to the create-vault button it shows "Your order" instead of "Group by action"
1.22.0 August 24, 2026
  • In the preview, Delete removes the selected clip: it leaves the reel and won't be exported. If it was the only one, the window closes
  • Fixed: after trimming a clip, playback goes back to where it was and respects whether it was paused. Before it started on its own from the beginning
1.21.0 August 24, 2026
  • A "Create vault" button inside the preview window: it creates the vault with what you're seeing, with the trims and the order you just set
  • It uses the same options you have set in the main window —timeline or composer, all cameras, group by action, output folder— and shows them next to the button so you don't have to go and check
1.20.0 August 24, 2026
  • Fixed: Shift now really does grab the start and the end of any clip, including at the junctions between two and at the ends of the strip
  • Moving a clip is now exclusively Option + drag. Without Option, dragging moves the playhead and never reorders by accident
  • At the junction between two clips, the edge you grab is the one belonging to the clip the mouse is over, not the neighbour's
1.19.0 August 24, 2026
  • Holding Shift brings up the handles on every clip: you can trim any of them by dragging its edge, without selecting it first
  • Holding Option, dragging a clip moves it, and the cursor says so
  • At each junction between two clips you now see both handles separately, the end of one and the start of the other
1.18.0 August 24, 2026
  • Fixed: you can now trim every clip, not just the first one. The edge handles were being covered by the clips that came after
  • Fixed: the playhead was showing behind the selected clip
  • Dragging the edges is smoother: while you move, only the change is drawn instead of redoing the whole strip every moment
  • The strip with the −5s and +5s buttons is bigger, so it's easier to see
1.17.0 August 24, 2026
  • You can lengthen or shorten each clip before exporting it. Pick a clip in the strip below and drag its edges, or use the −5s and +5s buttons for the start and the end. "Reset it" goes back to the original clip
  • You can change the order of the clips: drag one to another spot in the strip
  • All of this works the same for a single action's video and for the preview of the selection, and what you adjust is exactly what gets exported to the vault
1.16.0 August 24, 2026
  • Fixed: 1.15.0 couldn't talk to the server and was left unable to validate the license or check for updates. It now gets along with the server even when the server is on an earlier version
  • The installed version number is shown at the top right
  • A button to check for updates by hand, in case the automatic check failed
1.15.0 August 24, 2026
  • The update summary now appears in the app's language. Before it always came in Spanish because the server sent it untranslated
  • The error and progress messages that were missing are translated too
1.14.0 August 24, 2026
  • The what's-new page opens in the same language you're using the app in, with each version's notes translated
1.13.0 August 24, 2026
  • Info button at the top right: it opens the what's-new page with everything that changed in each version
1.12.0 August 24, 2026
  • Vault Finder speaks four languages: Spanish, English, French and Italian. The button at the top right shows which one it's in and lets you change it on the spot
  • The first time it starts in the system language, and you can leave it that way or pin one
1.11.0 August 24, 2026
  • Double-click the top strip to zoom the window or go back to its previous size, like any macOS window. It respects what you chose in System Settings: zoom, minimise or do nothing
1.10.0 August 24, 2026
  • The app tells you when the saved index was built by an earlier version, with a button to re-index right there. Before, after updating you had to remember to re-index, and without that the improvements didn't show
1.9.0 August 22, 2026
  • Fixed: composers using a video stored in ANOTHER vault in the same folder always came out with no video. They're found now. In a collection of 53 vaults, 130 lost actions turned up, spread across 7 vaults
  • After updating it's worth re-indexing to recover them
1.8.0 August 22, 2026
  • Vault Finder now has its own icon: you'll see it in the Dock, in Applications and when switching apps with Command + Tab
1.7.0 August 22, 2026
  • Fixed: some vaults were indexed entirely as "no video". Angles stores the videos in two different places depending on the machine, and Vault Finder only looked at one. It now finds both, so those vaults can be viewed and exported normally
1.6.0 August 22, 2026
  • Filters combine with AND, OR and NOT. Click the label between two filters to change it: "AND" requires both, "OR" needs just one, "NOT" leaves out the actions that have it
  • They read top to bottom, like a sentence: ONLY Try, OR Penalty, AND First half, NOT Error. Since the order matters, you can move a filter with the right button
  • The "Choose filters" button is now wider, so it doesn't look so tall
1.5.0 August 22, 2026
  • Updating is a single click: the app downloads the new version, installs it and restarts by itself. No more pressing "Restart" afterwards
  • If you're exporting or indexing, it waits until you're done: in that case it leaves the button so you can restart whenever you like
1.4.0 August 22, 2026
  • The expiry notice appears as soon as you activate the license, not the next time you open the app
  • The remaining days are counted by date, not by hours: a license expiring tomorrow says "expires tomorrow" at any time of day, and on the last day it says "expires today"
1.3.0 August 22, 2026
  • No more Keychain password prompts. The license key is now stored inside the app itself, so macOS never asks again — not even after an update
  • The license window no longer shows stale data when the key stops being valid: it says what happened instead of showing a client and an expiry that no longer hold
1.2.0 August 22, 2026
  • You can change the license from inside the app: when a notice appears at the top, there's a button next to the message to sort it out without leaving Vault Finder
  • License button always within reach, top right, even when there's no notice
  • You can release the Mac to move the license to another one: the slot frees up immediately and the key can be activated on the new machine
1.1.0 August 22, 2026
  • The app updates itself: on opening it tells you if there's a new version and installs it in one click
  • Per-client license, with an expiry date and a limit of Macs
  • Proof that the updater works end to end
1.0.0 August 22, 2026

First distributable version.

  • Searches actions across every Angles Vault in a folder: by action, qualifier, attribute, camera, stars or notes
  • Builds a new vault from the selection, as a timeline and/or composer
  • Takes every camera into the exported vault, in sync
  • Preview the selection without exporting anything, switching cameras with keys 1-9
  • When the export finishes, it opens the vault in Angles
